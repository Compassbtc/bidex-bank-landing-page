We may use information to operate and secure accounts, process transactions, verify identity, prevent fraud, provide support, improve our services, meet applicable obligations, resolve disputes, and send service updates or marketing messages where permitted.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our websites or application may use cookies and similar technologies to enable essential features, remember preferences, improve performance, and understand how services are used. We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, and meet applicable obligations. We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no online service or electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Please protect your account credentials and contact us if you suspect unauthorized activity.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may be able to request access to or correction or deletion of your information, object to or restrict certain processing, request a copy of your data, or withdraw consent where applicable. Links to third-party websites, wallets, or payment services are governed by those providers’ own privacy practices. Public blockchain transactions may expose wallet addresses and transaction details and may be permanent or impossible for BIDEX to remove.

8. Children and Policy Updates

Our services are not intended for children where their use is prohibited by applicable law. We may update this policy from time to time and will post the latest version here with a revised update date.
